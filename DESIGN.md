# Digital Audio Manager design brief

**Visual thesis:** a calm, dark listening desk. Graphite surfaces keep the tool focused; album art is the small saturated visual anchor; amber is reserved for actions and progress.

**Content plan:** navigation and collection scope live on the left, the selected playlist or queue is a dense readable table in the center, and the right rail contains the selected track, queue health, and device-sync impact.

**Interaction thesis:** every irreversible-feeling action is previewable. Downloads are explicit queue operations, while device changes begin with a diff and never touch files outside the managed root.
